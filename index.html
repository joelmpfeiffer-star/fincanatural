Our Story

A Rainforest in Motion

Finca Natural is a family-run off-grid regenerative farm carved into the hillside of Isla Solarte. Since 2021 we've been intentionally growing a young, evolving food forest — home to over 100 unique tropical and rare fruit species, wild-crafted herbal elixirs, fresh teas harvested from living trees, and a no-smell animal zone designed for harmony with visitors.

Every visit is a living, changing experience. The farm mimics a low-maintenance tropical rainforest ecosystem focused on education, transparency, and long-term ecological resilience.
