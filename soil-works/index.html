How It Works

Step 1: Contact Us

Reach out and tell us about your property and what you're trying to achieve. We'll discuss your project and determine if we're a good fit.

Step 2: Visit Finca Natural

For new clients, we require a visit to our farm first. Everything we do is rooted in soil biology, and the results of natural farming can be seen firsthand. This ensures we're aligned on approach and expectations.

Step 3: Site Visit

Once you've visited the farm, we'll schedule a site visit to your property to assess the land, understand your specific conditions, and design a custom program.

$150 Site Visit

(Credited toward your first payment if we move forward)

Three Pathways

We work in three core pathways depending on your land and goals. Each program is built on the same foundation: living soil, biological systems, and natural farming principles.

Restore & Nourish

Breathe life back into what's already there

For existing properties with trees or systems already in place. We prune, heal, apply biology, and train you to maintain thriving systems.

Best for: Existing properties with trees/systems in place

Design & Build

Start from scratch, do it right

Complete food system design and installation from the ground up. Site reading, species selection, layout, planting, and biological activation.

Best for: Raw land, new landowners, full system builds

Advanced Integration

Custom-tailored biology for maximum performance

Fully bespoke programs with advanced inputs: IMO systems, KNF, biochar, and custom biology tailored to your specific goals.

Best for: Maximum performance, rapid recovery, custom outcomes
