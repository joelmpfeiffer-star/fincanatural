Design & Build

Start from scratch, do it right

For Raw Land & New Systems

If you're starting with bare land, rebuilding after damage, or want a complete food system designed and installed from the ground up—this is the program.

We design and install complete food systems using natural farming principles. No guesswork. No generic templates. Every system is site-specific and built to work with your land's unique biology, water flow, sun patterns, and long-term goals.

The Process

1
Site Reading

We walk your land and observe: water movement, sun exposure, soil type, existing biology, wind patterns, and microclimates. The land tells us what it wants to become.

2
Species Selection & System Layout

Based on site conditions and your goals, we select species that thrive together. Layered canopy design, nitrogen-fixers, support species, and productive trees spaced for long-term growth.

3
On-Site Layout & Planting

We mark, dig, plant, and install the system. Depending on your program, this may include root inoculation with EM-1/JMS, mulch establishment, and companion planting for immediate biological activation.

4
Establishment Support & Training

Follow-up visits during the critical first months to ensure establishment. Hands-on training so you understand how to support the system as it matures. Ongoing care packages available if you want continued management.

What's Included

  • Complete site assessment and reading
  • Custom species selection and system design
  • Long-term spacing and layered canopy planning
  • On-site layout execution and planting
  • Biology applications and soil activation (as specified in your program)
  • Support systems tailored to your property
  • Establishment support and training

The Outcome

A biologically active system that establishes faster, produces earlier, and becomes self-sustaining. No fertilizers needed. The soil does the work.

Custom Pricing

Every property is different. Pricing depends on land size, system complexity, species selection, and installation scope.

Schedule a $150 site visit to assess your property and receive a detailed proposal.

Why Design Matters

Most food systems fail because they're planted without understanding the site. Trees spaced too close. Wrong species for the microclimate. No biology in the soil. No support systems.

We design for 10, 20, 50 years out. The system you get is built to mature into a productive, self-sustaining ecosystem that requires minimal intervention once established.
