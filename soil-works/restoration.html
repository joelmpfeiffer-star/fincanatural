Program 1

Restore & Nourish

Breathe life back into what's already there

For Existing Properties

If you have trees, gardens, or food systems already in place that need support—we can help bring them into balance and restore their natural productivity using biology-driven care.

We prune, shape, heal wounds, apply beneficial microorganisms, build soil biology, and train you to continue the work as your system matures.

4-Month Minimum Structure

Month 1 — Weekly Visits

Intensive hands-on work: assessment, pruning, wound treatment, biology application, mulching, and initial training.

Month 2 — Bi-Weekly Visits

Continued system support with biology reapplication, follow-up pruning, soil building, and deeper training.

Month 3–4 — Monthly Visits

Monitoring, final adjustments, advanced training, and system handoff preparation.

What's Included

  • Pruning and tree shaping (no rope climbing)
  • Removal of dead and diseased wood
  • Wound treatment using natural methods
  • EM-1 and JMS biology applications
  • Mulching and chop-and-drop
  • Soil building and system support
  • Hands-on training for continued maintenance

Program Pricing

$100–$200 per visit

Minimum 8 visits over 4 months

(Covers most residential properties)

What You Get

At the end of 4 months, your trees will be healthier, your soil biology will be starting to establish, and you'll have the knowledge to continue supporting the system as it matures.

This is the beginning—not the finish. Biology takes time to establish and spread. You'll start seeing benefits in the first few months, but the real transformation happens in years 2-3 as the system matures. By year 3-5, many systems are thriving with minimal intervention.

It's a slow burn, but it's the right way—and it works.

Long-term care packages are available for continued support beyond the 4-month program if you want ongoing management and monitoring as your system develops.

This is restoration work—not landscaping. We're rebuilding the biology that makes everything else possible.
