For Maximum Performance

This is a fully bespoke program built around your specific goals and property. Advanced inputs and biological systems are selected and integrated based on scope, materials, and client needs.

Not every system needs advanced biology. But when you want maximum results, rapid recovery, or fully customized outcomes—this is the program.

Available Advanced Integrations

These are not included by default—they're added based on your goals, property conditions, and system requirements.

IMO Systems

Indigenous Microorganism cultivation and application. Includes dry IMO4, liquid applications, and on-site IMO production systems.

KNF Inputs

Korean Natural Farming fermented plant juices (FPJ), oriental herbal nutrients (OHN), fish amino acids (FAA), and custom plant-based inputs.

Biochar & Carbon Systems

Biochar activation, carbonized rice husk (CRH) integration, and long-term carbon sequestration in soil systems.

Manure & Nutrient Cycling

Animal manure integration and closed-loop nutrient cycling for maximum biological activity.

No-Smell Animal Systems

Animal system design with biology to eliminate odor. Complete integration for productive, odor-free animal operations.

Animal Integration

Full animal system design, husbandry guidance, and integration into existing food systems.

Grafting & Air Layering

Advanced propagation techniques including grafting, air layering, and plant multiplication for system expansion.

Foliar Signaling Programs

Targeted leaf-applied biology to signal plant responses, accelerate growth, and enhance disease resistance.

Nursery Systems

On-site propagation infrastructure and nursery establishment for continuous system expansion.

Support Species Expansion

Nitrogen-fixing trees, support plants, guild installations, and system densification for accelerated maturity.

Erosion Control Systems

Vetiver installations, slope stabilization, and living erosion barriers integrated into productive systems.

Fully Custom Programs

Every Advanced Integration program is different. We assess your property, listen to your goals, and design a biological program tailored specifically to what you're trying to achieve.

This isn't a menu you pick from—it's a collaborative design process.

Custom Pricing

Pricing depends on scope, materials, labor, and ongoing system requirements.

Schedule a $150 site visit to discuss your goals and receive a detailed proposal.

Who This Is For

Advanced Integration is for clients who want to push systems beyond baseline productivity. Rapid recovery after disturbance. Maximum yield without fertilizers. Custom biology tailored to specific crops or conditions.

It's also for clients who understand that biology is the foundation and want to go deeper into microbial systems, advanced inputs, and regenerative techniques.

Long-Term Involvement

Depending on system complexity, ongoing involvement or deeper system understanding may be required long-term. Some advanced systems need periodic reapplication, monitoring, or adjustment as they evolve.

We design for handoff whenever possible, but the most advanced systems often benefit from continued partnership.
